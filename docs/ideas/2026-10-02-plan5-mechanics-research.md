# Plan 5 mechanics research: hidden plants and veil goggles (2026-10-02)

Research input for plan 5, not a spec. Design decisions from the author are taken as given (lichen on fir/pine trunks with three growth stages; mountain flower and ashlands fern as rare ground vegetation; three helmet-slot goggles, level N reveals plants <= N, level III also cancels the tier III hidden-from-players effect for the wearer).

Citation style: `File.cs:line` means `tools/decompiled/assembly_valheim/File.cs` (game 1.0.16). `(?)` means unverified. Jötunn 2.30.2 facts come from decompiling `BepInEx/plugins/Jotunn/Jotunn.dll` with ilspycmd (no URL; local DLL). The prior-art doc `2026-10-02-asset-pipeline-research.md` named in the brief does not exist in `docs/ideas/`, so §4 of it was not read.

## 1. Pickable, Plant and the Cultivator

### 1.1 Pickable lifecycle

- Class: `public class Pickable : MonoBehaviour, Hoverable, Interactable` (Pickable.cs:4). It looks up its ZNetView with `GetComponent<ZNetView>()` on its own GameObject (Pickable.cs:80), not in parents. A Pickable placed on a child of another ZNetView object would get a null view and throw in `Awake` (it dereferences `m_nview.GetZDO()` on line 81). So a vanilla `Pickable` can only live on a root with its own ZNetView.
- Inspector fields that matter: `m_hideWhenPicked` (GameObject toggled with SetActive), `m_itemPrefab`, `m_amount`, `m_respawnTimeMinutes`, `m_respawnTimeInitMin/Max`, `m_defaultPicked`, `m_defaultEnabled`, `m_extraDrops`, `m_pickEffector`, `m_pickRaiseSkill` (Pickable.cs:8-62), `m_loopAudioSource` (Pickable.cs:12; not touched by Pickable itself after Awake, so a gate must handle audio).
- `Awake` (Pickable.cs:78-110): registers RPCs `RPC_SetPicked` (bool) and `RPC_Pick` (int); reads ZDO keys `picked` (`ZDOVars.s_picked`, ZDOVars.cs:209) and `picked_time` (`s_pickedTime`, ZDOVars.cs:9) and `enabled` (`s_enabled`, ZDOVars.cs:99); calls `m_hideWhenPicked.SetActive(!m_picked && m_enabled == 1)` (Pickable.cs:98); starts `InvokeRepeating("UpdateRespawn", 1-5 s, 60 s)` only if `m_respawnTimeMinutes > 0` (Pickable.cs:103). If there is no respawn time, no hide object and it was picked, the ZDO is destroyed (Pickable.cs:105-109).
- `GetHoverText` returns `""` when picked or disabled (Pickable.cs:114-120). `Interact` (Pickable.cs:180) sends `RPC_Pick` to the ZDO owner via `m_nview.InvokeRPC("RPC_Pick", bonus)`.
- `RPC_Pick` (Pickable.cs:235, runs only on the owner): creates effects, instantiates `m_itemPrefab` stacks via `Drop` (Pickable.cs:331, needs a Rigidbody on the item prefab), then `InvokeRPC(ZNetView.Everybody, "RPC_SetPicked", true)`.
- `SetPicked(bool)` (Pickable.cs:269): sets `m_hideWhenPicked.SetActive(!picked)` on every client (Pickable.cs:274); the owner writes `picked` and, if a respawn time exists, `picked_time = ZNet.GetTime().Ticks` (Pickable.cs:279-287). With no respawn time and no hide object it destroys the ZDO (Pickable.cs:289-292).
- `UpdateRespawn` (Pickable.cs:132, owner only): when `ShouldRespawn()` (elapsed world time since `picked_time` > `m_respawnTimeMinutes`, Pickable.cs:160-176) it broadcasts `RPC_SetPicked(false)`.
- How it hides: only through `m_hideWhenPicked.SetActive`. There is no renderer or collider toggling. Because the collider normally sits under `m_hideWhenPicked`, deactivating it removes hover and interaction.
- Growth stages: Pickable has none. It has exactly two states (ripe / picked). It does support regrowth (timestamp-based, so it also advances while the zone was unloaded and fires on the next `UpdateRespawn`).

### 1.2 Plant (farmed crops, saplings)

- `public class Plant : SlowUpdate, Hoverable` (Plant.cs:4). Fields: `m_growTime`, `m_growTimeMax`, `m_grownPrefabs`, `m_minScale/m_maxScale`, `m_growRadius`, `m_needCultivatedGround`, `m_destroyIfCantGrow`, `m_tolerateHeat`, `m_tolerateCold`, `[BitMask] m_biome`, private `m_healthy/m_unhealthy/m_healthyGrown/m_unhealthyGrown` (Plant.cs:24-61).
- `Awake` (Plant.cs:92): `GetComponent<ZNetView>()` on the same object; stores `seed` (= `(int)(m_uid.ID + m_uid.UserID)`, line 101) and `plantTime` (`ZDOVars.s_plantTime`, ZDOVars.cs:11) in the ZDO on the owner.
- `SUpdate` every 10 s (Plant.cs:144-170): `UpdateHealth`, then swaps visual children: before 50 % of the grow time `m_healthy`/`m_unhealthy`, after 50 % `m_healthyGrown`/`m_unhealthyGrown` (Plant.cs:152-159). When `timeSincePlanted > GetGrowTime()` and the caller is the owner, `Grow()` runs. `GetGrowTime` is `Lerp(m_growTime, m_growTimeMax, seededRandom)` per plant (Plant.cs:172-179).
- `Grow` (Plant.cs:181-213): picks a random entry of `m_grownPrefabs`, `Instantiate`s it at the plant position, then `m_nview.Destroy()` the plant. The grown prefab gets its own new ZDO (it has its own ZNetView).
- `UpdateHealth` (Plant.cs:215-263): status `WrongBiome` if `(biome & m_biome) == 0`; `NotCultivated` if `m_needCultivatedGround` and `!heightmap.IsCultivated`; `TooHot` in AshLands unless `m_tolerateHeat` or inside a shield; `TooCold` in DeepNorth/Mountain unless `m_tolerateCold`; `NoSun` if a raycast up 100 m hits layers `Default/static_solid/piece` (Plant.cs:376-386); `NoSpace` if another healthy Plant is within `m_growRadius`. Any non-Healthy status stops growth (Grow returns null, Plant.cs:183-190).
- **The lichen's three stages as Plant stages**: yes for the ground (flat) variant. Plant already has two visual stages (young, half-grown) plus the grown prefab, which can be a `Pickable` (stage 3). Settings for our plants: Mountains flower `m_biome = Mountain`, `m_tolerateCold = true`; Ashlands fern `m_biome = AshLands`, `m_tolerateHeat = true`; lichen `m_biome = BlackForest`. Risk: `HaveRoof` (Plant.cs:376) may report NoSun under a forest canopy if tree crowns have colliders on those layers (?) - spike. After a pick, a Pickable only regrows its ripe visual; it does not go back to stage 1 (regrowth is fine, stage replay is not).
- **Plant cannot be used for the tree lichen**: `Grow` destroys the owner of the Plant's ZNetView (`m_nview.Destroy()`, Plant.cs:207-209) and `Destroy()` damages the root `IDestructible` (Plant.cs:365-374), which for a nested component would be the tree.

### 1.3 Cultivator placement

- Pieces come from the item's `m_buildPieces` PieceTable (see `Humanoid.SetupEquipment`, Humanoid.cs:1437-1444). `PieceTable.UpdateAvailable` (PieceTable.cs:42-92) lists a piece only if `piece.m_enabled` and its `m_name` is in `knownRecipies` (PieceTable.cs:75; `(?)` how a custom piece becomes known: normally by picking up its resource items - spike), then sorts by `Piece.m_category` (enum with `Misc = 0` first, Piece.cs:53-66).
- Placement rules on the Piece: `m_groundOnly`, `m_cultivatedGroundOnly` (Piece.cs:145-147), `m_onlyInBiome` (Piece.cs:208). `Player` checks `m_cultivatedGroundOnly && !heightmap.IsCultivated(point)` -> `NeedCultivated` (Player.cs:3901-3904) and `m_onlyInBiome` -> `WrongBiome` (Player.cs:4085-4088). Hiding the placement marker for these piece types: Player.cs:3809.
- Jötunn: the string `_CultivatorPieceTable` exists in `Jotunn.dll` (`Jotunn.Configs.PieceConfig.PieceTable`, string compare via ilspy strings). `PieceManager.AddPiece(new CustomPiece(prefab, fixReference, new PieceConfig { PieceTable = "_CultivatorPieceTable", Category = "Misc", Requirements = ... }))` (?) - the class/field names are from the Jötunn docs https://valheim-modding.github.io/Jotunn/tutorials/pieces.html and were not re-checked in the DLL. The planted prefab needs `Piece` (with `m_cultivatedGroundOnly`), `ZNetView`, `Plant` and an `IDestructible` (vanilla saplings use `Destructible`; Plant.Destroy needs one, Plant.cs:365-374).

## 2. Tree prefabs and a nested lichen

### 2.1 Tree identification

- Black Forest firs and pines are `TreeBase` (Plant.cs:206 even calls `GetComponent<TreeBase>()`). `TreeBase` fields: `m_trunk`, `m_stubPrefab`, `m_logPrefab`, `m_logSpawnPoint`, `m_health`, `m_minToolTier`, `m_dropWhenDestroyed` (TreeBase.cs:5-43).
- Damage: `Damage` -> `InvokeRPC("RPC_Damage")` (TreeBase.cs:62-65), `RPC_Damage` runs on the owner; at health <= 0 it calls `SpawnLog` (log prefab + optional stub, TreeBase.cs:156, 225-246), then `gameObject.SetActive(false)` and `m_nview.Destroy()` (TreeBase.cs:165-166). The whole tree hierarchy, including any child we add, goes with it.
- Shake animation rotates `m_trunk.transform.localRotation` for 1 s (TreeBase.cs:208-223); grow animation clones `m_trunk` and scales the clone (TreeBase.cs:77-99).
- Prefab names: decompiled code does not contain them (assets are not decompiled). The local SoftRef asset-name list `valheim_Data/StreamingAssets/SoftRef/manifest_extended` contains `FirTree`, `FirTree_small`, `FirTree_small_dead`, `FirTree_big`, `FirTree_snowfall` (snow variant), `Pinetree_01`, `PineTree_01_dead`, `Pine_tree_normal_small`, plus `_log/_Stub` variants (?: asset names in that list are not guaranteed to be root prefab names; check with `ip_prefabs fir` / `ip_prefabs pine` in game). Black Forest also has `Birch`/`Oak` leftovers from other biomes; they are out of scope.
- Hierarchy contents (trunk collider layer, LODGroup, number of children) are not visible from the decompile (?) - dump in game.

### 2.2 Adding a child to a vanilla prefab (Jötunn)

- `PrefabManager.OnVanillaPrefabsAvailable` (public static event, Jotunn.dll `Jotunn.Managers.PrefabManager`) fires when vanilla prefabs can be fetched with `PrefabManager.Instance.GetPrefab(name)` (this repo already uses that event for the potions; `Items/PotionItems.cs`). Instantiating our lichen asset under the vanilla prefab (`Object.Instantiate(lichen, treePrefab.transform)`) is an in-memory change of the prefab; every later `Instantiate` by `ZNetScene.CreateObject` (ZNetScene.cs:92-116) and by `ZoneSystem.PlaceVegetation` (ZoneSystem.cs:1551) carries the child, on all clients that run the mod, in existing worlds, for already generated trees as well (trees are recreated from ZDOs). Clients without the mod simply see an unchanged tree.
- Cost: the child component exists on every tree instance in loaded zones, so its `Awake` must be cheap and trees that lose the roll must destroy the child in the same frame.

### 2.3 Nested ZNetView: do not

- `ZNetView.Awake` consumes the static `m_initZDO` once (ZNetView.cs:55-58), set by `ZNetScene.CreateObject` right before `Instantiate` (ZNetScene.cs:103-112). A second ZNetView in the hierarchy finds `m_initZDO == null`; with `m_useInitZDO` set it logs "Double ZNetview when initializing object" (ZNetView.cs:51-54) and then falls through to the `else` branch that calls `ZDOMan.CreateNewZDO` (ZNetView.cs:88-100): a new, unrelated persistent ZDO per client per instantiation. So a nested ZNetView would leak ZDOs and never sync. Not usable.
- The vanilla pattern is the opposite: child components use `GetComponentInParent<ZNetView>()` and the root's ZDO. Examples: `MaterialVariation` (MaterialVariation.cs:35, and it registers an RPC on the parent's view at line 46), `RandomMaterialValues` (RandomMaterialValues.cs:55, stores a seed in the parent ZDO at 68-72), `SpawnPrefab` (SpawnPrefab.cs:11, stores `HasSpawned_<name>` in the parent ZDO at 28), also `ObjectSwitcher.cs:40`, `Smelter.cs:90`, `OfferingBowl.cs:92`.
- `ZNetView.Register` uses `Dictionary.Add` (ZNetView.cs:275 ff.), so an RPC name registered twice on one view throws. Use a unique name (e.g. `IP_LichenPick`) and register once per tree. Calls route by the root's `ZDOID` and handler hash (`InvokeRPC`, ZNetView.cs:326-329 in the numbering of the listing; `HandleRoutedRPC` ZNetView.cs:314-325) so the RPC executes on the tree ZDO owner.
- Ghost spawn: `ZoneSystem.SpawnZone` with `SpawnMode.Ghost` instantiates trees under `ZNetView.StartGhostInit` and destroys them immediately (ZoneSystem.cs:1547-1566, 1337-1352). The lichen component must not write ZDO data in `Awake`/first frame, and should do its work in `Start` (which a destroyed ghost never reaches) like `SpawnPrefab`/`RandomMaterialValues` do.

### 2.4 Deterministic "this tree has lichen"

- ZDOID is not a stable seed for persistent decisions. `ZDO.Load` reassigns IDs on every world load: `m_uid.SetID(++ZDOID.m_loadID)` (ZDO.cs:1298) with the user key set to "unknown former user" (ZDOID.cs:73), so the numeric ID depends on load order and changes after destroyed objects, a server restart or a save/load. Within one session all peers agree on the ID, but the roll would change per restart and the lichen would appear and vanish. `Plant` avoids this by storing the seed in the ZDO at first sight (Plant.cs:98-103); doing that for every tree would write a key to every tree ZDO.
- Recommended seed: the tree's world position. Trees never move; the position is part of the ZDO and identical on all clients and across restarts. Quantize (for example `RoundToInt(x * 10)`, `RoundToInt(z * 10)`), mix with a mod-specific salt in an integer hash (splitmix/xorshift; do not use `string.GetHashCode` or `UnityEngine.Random` state), and compare to the configured probability. This is also what vanilla does for vegetation: `ZoneSystem.PlaceVegetation` seeds `Random.InitState(worldSeed + zone.x*4271 + zone.y*9187 + prefabNameHash)` (ZoneSystem.cs:1397). Include `WorldGenerator.instance.GetSeed()` (ZoneSystem.cs:1388) in the hash so different worlds differ.
- Two trees at the same quantized position are practically impossible (tree spacing); duplicates would only share a roll.
- Persistent lichen state (planted/last-picked timestamp) goes into the tree's ZDO under custom keys, written by the owner only when something changes (first pick). A tree with no keys is "ripe, never picked", so the mod writes nothing for the millions of unpicked trees.
- Growth stages derived from a timestamp: the visual stage is `f(now - lastPickedTicks)` evaluated on every client from the ZDO (`ZNet.instance.GetTime()`, ZNet.cs:2802, same clock Pickable and Plant use, Pickable.cs:165, Plant.cs:141). No owner-side timer or RPC is needed for growth and regrowth; only the pick RPC mutates state. This also makes regrowth work while the zone was unloaded, which `Pickable`'s `InvokeRepeating` also achieves, but with a 60 s poll on the owner only.

## 3. Hiding without a trace (client side)

- Hover: `Player.FindHoverObject` raycasts 50 m on `m_interactMask` (layers item, piece, piece_nonsolid, Default, static_solid, Default_small, character, character_net, terrain, vehicle, character_ghost; Player.cs:671, 4259-4263), takes the first hit in distance order and breaks (Player.cs:4263-4300). The Hud asks `hoverObject.GetComponentInParent<Hoverable>().GetHoverText()` (Hud.cs:829-831) and `Player.Interact` uses `GetComponentInParent<Interactable>()` (Player.cs:4306-4309).
- Consequences: (a) a disabled collider is invisible to `Physics.Raycast`, so the ray reaches the trunk/terrain behind it and neither hover text nor interaction can start; this alone covers both; (b) an enabled hidden collider on an item layer would still block the ray and show an empty crosshair state (only if the object has Hoverable text `""` it shows nothing, but the object still "eats" the ray); so use `Collider.enabled = false`, not just empty hover text; (c) because hover is resolved with `GetComponentInParent`, a Hoverable on a child of the tree does not make the tree itself hoverable.
- Visuals: toggle `Renderer.enabled` (mesh, skinned, particle, trail), `Light.enabled`, and any looping `AudioSource` (`Pickable.m_loopAudioSource`, Pickable.cs:12). Do not use `GameObject.SetActive` on objects Pickable also drives: `m_hideWhenPicked.SetActive` is set in `Awake`, `SetPicked`, `SetEnabled`, `UpdateRespawn` (Pickable.cs:98, 274, 314, 151). Renderer/collider `enabled` flags survive SetActive cycles, so a gate and Pickable do not fight.
- Implementation shape: one `VeilSight` MonoBehaviour per plant (`RequiredLevel` 1..3) that caches `GetComponentsInChildren<Renderer/Collider/Light/AudioSource>(true)` once, subscribes to a static `GogglesLevelChanged` event, and applies state on enable and on change. No per-frame cost. Same component for tree-nested lichen and for ground plants. For ground plants the root also holds `Pickable` and `ZNetView` and the gate does not touch the root.
- Particles and shadows: `Renderer.enabled = false` removes the renderer's shadow too; `ParticleSystem` keeps simulating but nothing is drawn; for cleanliness also `Stop(true, Clear)` / `Play()` on level change.
- Tree LOD: `TreeBase` looks up an optional `LODGroup` on the root (TreeBase.cs:81-85). A child renderer that is not listed in the LODGroup is drawn at all distances while the tree swaps to LOD1/billboards (?). Either add the lichen renderers to the group's LOD0 (inverse of `Utils.RemoveFromLodgroup`, used in VisEquipment.cs:1020, 1069, 1201), or gate by distance (for example reveal only within 30 m during the gate's poll). Verify the hierarchy in game.
- Tree identity: the nested child must not change the trunk look. With the roll destroying the child at `Start`, a no-lichen tree is byte-identical to vanilla at runtime. A tree that has lichen but is not revealed has an inactive-looking child (renderers off, collider off); nothing differs for the player.
- Parenting choice (spike): under the root, the lichen does not move when the trunk shakes (m_trunk.localRotation +-1.5 deg, TreeBase.cs:208-223); under `m_trunk` it shakes along but `GrowAnimation` clones the trunk including the child (TreeBase.cs:79).
- Server-side: the plant exists in the ZDO and the object is instantiated on every client (including its hidden collider and renderers). A modified client or a ZDO inspector can see and pick it. Acceptable for a PvE-focused mod, because (a) goggles are an in-game gate, not a security boundary; (b) the real position leak of the player under the spoof is a more sensitive case and is handled server side (see §6).

## 4. Vegetation spawning

### 4.1 ZoneVegetation and PlaceVegetation

Fields (ZoneSystem.cs:34-125): `m_prefab`, `m_enable`, `m_min`, `m_max`, `m_forcePlacement`, `m_scaleMin/Max`, `m_randTilt`, `m_chanceToUseGroundTilt`, `m_biome` (mask), `m_biomeArea` (Edge=1, Median=2, Everything=3; Heightmap.cs:48-53), `m_blockCheck`, `m_snapToStaticSolid`, `m_minAltitude/m_maxAltitude`, `m_minVegetation/m_maxVegetation`, `m_surroundCheck*`, `m_minOceanDepth/Max`, `m_minTilt/m_maxTilt`, `m_terrainDeltaRadius/m_minTerrainDelta/m_maxTerrainDelta`, `m_snapToWater`, `m_groundOffset`, `m_groupSizeMin/Max`, `m_groupRadius`, `m_minDistanceFromCenter/Max`, `m_inForest`, `m_forestTresholdMin/Max`.

`PlaceVegetation` (ZoneSystem.cs:1385-1589), per zone (64 m) and per entry:
- Skips if `!m_enable` or the zone heightmap does not contain the biome (`hmap.HaveBiome`, line 1393).
- Seed: `worldSeed + zone.x*4271 + zone.y*9187 + prefabName.GetStableHashCode()` (line 1397): deterministic, and different per prefab name.
- **Rarity**: `if (m_max < 1) { if (Random.value > m_max) continue; }` with the count fixed to 1 (lines 1399-1405), else `Random.Range((int)m_min, (int)m_max + 1)` (line 1408). So `m_max = 0.05` means "5 % of qualifying zones get exactly one placement attempt batch", and `m_min` is ignored in that branch. This is the per-zone probability field; there is no other one. Values >= 1 are truncated to int, so the lowest non-zero "guaranteed" density is 1 per zone.
- Each attempt picks a random point, applies `m_blockCheck` (location/building block), then ground data biome and area (1438-1442), altitude (`p.y - 30` vs min/max, 1448-1452), vegetation mask, ocean depth, tilt (1469), terrain delta, distance from center, forest factor (`WorldGenerator.GetForestFactor`, 1489-1496), clear areas (1524). `m_groupSizeMin/Max` and `m_groupRadius` make clusters around the first point (1425-1429); `m_forcePlacement` multiplies attempts by 50 (1415).
- Prefabs with a ZNetView are instantiated only in `Full` or `Ghost` mode (1543-1568), i.e. on the server for zones that are not yet generated. Prefabs without a ZNetView are plain local objects (1570-1575).
- Zones already generated are never repopulated: `SpawnZone` places vegetation only if `!IsZoneGenerated(zoneID)` (ZoneSystem.cs:1337), and the host/server uses `Client` mode for generated zones (ZoneSystem.cs:1279). **Consequence: custom ground vegetation (plants 2 and 3) appears only in not-yet-generated zones of a world; the tree-nested lichen works in all existing worlds because trees are recreated from the prefab.** The dedicated server must run the mod for ground vegetation to be generated.
- Biome enum (Heightmap.cs:16-30): `Meadows = 1`, `Swamp = 2`, `Mountain = 4`, `BlackForest = 8`, `Plains = 0x10`, `AshLands = 0x20`, `DeepNorth = 0x40`, `Ocean = 0x100`, `Mistlands = 0x200`.

### 4.2 Jötunn mapping

- `ZoneManager.Instance.AddCustomVegetation(new CustomVegetation(prefab, fixReference, new VegetationConfig { ... }))` (`Jotunn.Managers.ZoneManager.AddCustomVegetation`, `Jotunn.Entities.CustomVegetation`). Events: `ZoneManager.OnVanillaVegetationAvailable`, `OnVegetationRegistered`.
- `VegetationConfig` exposes exactly: `Biome`, `BiomeArea` (default 3), `BlockCheck` (true), `ForcePlacement`, `Min`, `Max` (default 10), `MinAltitude`, `MaxAltitude`, `MinOceanDepth`, `MaxOceanDepth`, `MinTerrainDelta`, `MaxTerrainDelta`, `TerrainDeltaRadius`, `MinTilt`, `MaxTilt`, `InForest`, `ForestThresholdMin/Max`, `ScaleMin/Max`, `GroupSizeMin/Max`, `GroupRadius`, `GroundOffset`. Not exposed: `m_chanceToUseGroundTilt`, `m_randTilt`, `m_snapToWater`, `m_snapToStaticSolid`, `m_minVegetation/m_maxVegetation`, `m_surroundCheck*`, distance-from-center. If any of those is needed, fetch the created entry with `ZoneManager.Instance.GetZoneVegetation(name)` and set the field directly (the public `ZoneVegetation` object, Jotunn `GetZoneVegetation` at ZoneManager line 237 of the decompile).
- `Min/Max` map straight onto `m_min/m_max`, so `Max = 0.05f` is the rarity switch described above.
- Suggested starting configs (all values are tuning guesses, `(?)` in-game density spike): fern `Biome = AshLands, Max = 0.3f, GroupSizeMin/Max = 1/1, MinTilt = 0, MaxTilt = 30`; flower `Biome = Mountain, Max = 0.25f, MinAltitude` above snow line (?); flat lichen `Biome = BlackForest, InForest = true, Max = 0.2f` (ground variant is mainly obtained by cultivation, wild placement optional).

## 5. Equipment effects and the goggles item

- Equipping: `Humanoid.EquipItem` sets `m_helmetItem` for `ItemType.Helmet` after unequipping the old one (Humanoid.cs:1226-1234), then `SetupEquipment` runs (Humanoid.cs:1428-1445) -> `SetupVisEquipment` (helmet hash = `m_helmetItem.m_dropPrefab.name.GetStableHashCode()`, Humanoid.cs:1417) and `UpdateEquipmentStatusEffects` (Humanoid.cs:1483-1517).
- `UpdateEquipmentStatusEffects` builds a set from every equipped item's `m_shared.m_equipStatusEffect` and set effect (Humanoid.cs:1502-1505, 1536-1537), calls `m_seman.RemoveStatusEffect(NameHash)` for removed ones and `m_seman.AddStatusEffect(effect, resetTime: false)` for new ones (Humanoid.cs:1555-1564). So an equip SE appears on the wearer exactly while the helmet is worn (and is re-applied on login via the same path; confirm in game).
- Note for goggles: the SE is optional. The simpler and cheaper truth source is the local item: `Player.m_localPlayer.m_helmetItem` (`protected` in Humanoid.cs:85; the project builds with publicized assemblies, `Properties/IgnoreAccessModifiers.cs`), compare `m_dropPrefab.name` to the three goggle prefab names or read a `GogglesLevel` field from a small `ItemDrop` component. Poll at 0.5-1 s in one manager and raise `GogglesLevelChanged` on change. Optionally hook `Humanoid.UpdateEquipmentStatusEffects` (private; patchable; adds a patch target to `Plugin.ExpectedPatchTargets`) for instant reaction. An equip SE adds a HUD icon and tooltip text for free (`m_equipStatusEffect.m_name` in ItemDrop.cs:1110-1113); then the level can also be read through `SEMan.HaveStatusEffect`.
- Visuals: `VisEquipment.SetHelmetItem` -> `SetHelmetEquipped` (VisEquipment.cs:1170-1186): `AttachItem(hash, 0, m_helmet)`. `AttachItem` (VisEquipment.cs:1347-1415) looks for a direct child of the item prefab named `attach` (or `attach_skin`, which is skinned to the body), instantiates it, disables its colliders (`CleanupInstance`, VisEquipment.cs:1417-1423), parents it to `m_helmet` joint and applies an optional `equipoffset` child (VisEquipment.cs:1356, 1407-1411). So the goggles prefab must contain `attach` (mesh at the head joint origin). Hair/beard hiding: `m_helmetHideHair` default `Hidden`, `m_helmetHideBeard` default `Default` (ItemDrop.cs:197-199, enum values Default/Hidden/HiddenHat/HiddenHood/HiddenNeck/HiddenScarf, ItemDrop.cs:68-75); for glasses use `m_helmetHideHair = Default` so the hairstyle stays.
- Other players see the helmet through the replicated visual hash, so goggles render on remote players without extra work.
- Levels: either three distinct items (own prefab name, mesh, icon and recipe each; `ItemType.Helmet` with unique `m_name`), or one item with `m_maxQuality = 3` (ItemDrop.cs:116) and `ItemData.m_quality` (ItemDrop.cs:421) as the level; quality upgrades use the vanilla workbench upgrade flow and the same visual for all levels. Three distinct items are the better fit: each tier gets its own model (asset pipeline), tier-matched ingredients, the check is a name compare, and the upgrade is just a new craft that consumes the previous goggles as an ingredient. Quality would only hold up if all three levels share one mesh.
- Jötunn: `new CustomItem("VeilGogglesI", "HelmetLeather"?, itemConfig)` clones a vanilla helmet prefab (?; pattern as in `Items/PotionItems.cs`), then replace the `attach` child with our mesh. Armor value and durability can stay near zero (`m_armor`, `m_useDurability` false).

## 6. Revealing tier III hidden players for the goggle wearer

Existing code touchpoints (this repo):
- `HiddenState.IsHiddenFromPlayers(Character)` (`InvisibilityPotion/Net/HiddenState.cs:45`) and the ZDO overload (`HiddenState.cs:52`).
- Viewer-side nameplate: `PlayerHidePatches.TestShow_Postfix` sets `__result = false` for `IsHiddenFromPlayers(c)` (`Patches/PlayerHidePatches.cs:21-25`).
- Server-side map list: `UpdatePlayerList_Postfix` clears `m_publicPosition` and `m_position` (`PlayerHidePatches.cs:27-44`).
- Server-side header position spoof: `SendZDOs` transpiler -> `HeaderPosition(ZDO, peer)` returns `(x, 10000, z)` for hidden players unless the peer owns the ZDO (`PlayerHidePatches.cs:84-124`).
- Viewer-side body: `VeilController.Refresh` computes `forceHide = !isLocal && HiddenState.IsHiddenFromPlayers(p)` (`Visuals/VeilController.cs:154`) and `FogVeil.Apply` then draws nothing for it (`Visuals/FogVeil.cs:198-202, 216`).

Where a "viewer wears level III goggles" exception plugs in:

1. **Nameplate** (client only, easy): in `TestShow_Postfix`, skip the `__result = false` when `GogglesLevel.Local >= 3`.
2. **Body hide** (client only, easy): in `VeilController.Refresh`, `forceHide = !isLocal && IsHiddenFromPlayers(p) && GogglesLevel.Local < 3`. The veil mode for the wearer is then the normal tier III look (or a dedicated "seen through goggles" look, for example the faint Tint/ghost mode, since the real intent is "I can see a shimmering figure"). Refresh runs on a timer in `VeilController.Update`, so a goggle change is picked up without extra hooks.
3. **Position** (server side, but per viewer, not global): the spoof is not global. `HeaderPosition` already receives the receiving peer and compares it to the ZDO owner (`PlayerHidePatches.cs:119-123`), and `ZNetPeer.m_characterID` maps a peer to its player ZDOID (ZNetPeer.cs:21; ZNet.cs:2113 sets it). So the server can return the real position when the receiving peer's own player ZDO says it wears level III goggles: the wearer's client writes an int key `IP_Goggles` on its own player ZDO (same pattern as `HiddenState.Write`), the server reads `ZDOMan.instance.GetZDO(peer.m_characterID).GetInt(IP_Goggles)` inside `HeaderPosition` and skips the spoof if >= 3. Without this change the wearer's client receives the spoofed y=10000 position, so any body or fog drawn for the hidden player would be 10 km up and effectively not shown (the wearer would "see" a nameplate hovering at y=10000 at best, and the nameplate would never show since the distance check fails). With it, the wearer sees the player at the real, live position (only as live as other players are).
4. **Map pin** (server side, global package): `SendPlayerList` builds one `PlayerInfo` list and sends the identical `ZPackage` to every peer (ZNet.cs:2517-2532), and the mod mutates the list itself in `UpdatePlayerList`. A per-viewer map pin therefore requires either a prefix on `SendPlayerList` that builds the package per peer, or skipping pins for goggle wearers entirely. Recommendation: no map pin for hidden players even for goggles wearers in v1 (goggles show them in the world, not on the map); revisit only if testers ask.
5. **AI and cost**: HiddenState reads in the hot AI paths are unaffected (the exception is only on the player-vs-player surfaces above). The goggles-level int is read once per header only for hidden players on the server.
6. Cheat note: the level is client-asserted. Acceptable here (same trust level as the existing `IP_Hidden` ZDO keys).

What the wearer sees if only the viewer-side parts (1 and 2) are implemented: the position stays spoofed for everybody except the owner (`HeaderPosition`), so the body is at y=10000 and not visible; on a listen host the body is at the real position anyway (VeilController comment, `VeilController.cs:151-153`). So part 3 is required for a real reveal.

## 7. Recommended mechanics

**Plant 1, Huldra's lichen (Black Forest, on trees).** Add a child prefab `IP_Lichen` to the vanilla Black Forest tree prefabs (`FirTree`, `FirTree_small`, `Pinetree_01`, ... after verifying names with `ip_prefabs`) in `PrefabManager.OnVanillaPrefabsAvailable`, excluding `*_dead` and log/stub prefabs. The child carries one custom component `TreeLichen` that finds `GetComponentInParent<ZNetView>()` and the parent `TreeBase`, rolls `hash(worldSeed, quantized position, salt) < LichenChance` in `Start` (destroy the child on a failed roll), reads `IP_LichenT` (long ticks of last pick, absent = ripe) from the tree ZDO each poll, derives stage 1 / 2 / 3 from `now - IP_LichenT` against configured minutes, and swaps three child meshes by `Renderer.enabled`. A second component `VeilSight(RequiredLevel = 1)` toggles all renderers, lights and colliders by `GogglesLevel.Local`. Interaction: `TreeLichen` implements `Hoverable`/`Interactable` on the child; only stage 3 is pickable; `Interact` sends `IP_LichenPick` through the parent view (registered once), the owner checks stage, spawns the drop like `Pickable.Drop` (Pickable.cs:331) and writes `IP_LichenT = now`. The tree's ZDO is untouched until the first pick. Felling the tree destroys the lichen with it (TreeBase.cs:165-166). The flat ground variant (cultivation) is a separate prefab: `ZNetView` + `Plant` (`m_biome = BlackForest`, two visual stages, `m_grownPrefabs = [IP_LichenGroundPickable]`) + `Piece` (`m_cultivatedGroundOnly`, Jötunn Cultivator table) and a `Pickable` (respawn time, `m_hideWhenPicked`) as the ripe stage; both prefabs carry the same `VeilSight`.

**Plant 2, Mountains flower, and Plant 3, Ashlands fern.** Standalone prefabs: `ZNetView` (persistent) + `Pickable` (`m_itemPrefab` = plant item, `m_hideWhenPicked` = the flower/fern mesh child, `m_respawnTimeMinutes` = regrowth, `m_respawnTimeInitMin/Max` to desynchronise) + `VeilSight(RequiredLevel = 2 or 3)` on the root, which gates renderers, lights, particle systems, `m_loopAudioSource` and colliders. Spawn through `ZoneManager.AddCustomVegetation` with `VegetationConfig { Biome = Mountain / AshLands, Max < 1 (rarity), GroupSize 1, altitude / tilt limits }`; optionally also cultivable via `Plant` (`m_biome` and `m_tolerateCold` / `m_tolerateHeat`) so players can farm them in their own biome (matches the picked-state and regrowth wishes without extra code). Remember the existing-world limitation (§4.1): only newly generated zones get wild specimens; cultivated ones work everywhere.

**Goggles.** Three `ItemType.Helmet` items (`VeilGogglesI/II/III`, cloned from a vanilla helmet in `OnVanillaPrefabsAvailable`, with an `attach` child mesh, `m_helmetHideHair = Default`), crafted at tier-matched stations; each upgrade recipe consumes the previous level. A static `GogglesLevel` service reads `Player.m_localPlayer.m_helmetItem?.m_dropPrefab.name` (0.5-1 s poll, plus optional postfix on `Humanoid.UpdateEquipmentStatusEffects`) and raises `GogglesLevelChanged`; every `VeilSight` re-evaluates only on that event. Level III also: the viewer-side nameplate and `VeilController` exceptions (§6.1-2), a new player-ZDO key `IP_Goggles` written by the wearer's client, and a server-side exception in `PlayerHidePatches.HeaderPosition` that returns the real position for a peer whose player ZDO has `IP_Goggles >= 3` (§6.3). No map pin for hidden players, even with goggles (§6.4). Optional `SE_VeilSight` as `m_equipStatusEffect` for HUD icon and tooltip.
